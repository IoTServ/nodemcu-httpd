中文

我们重视并保护您的隐私。本政策说明【邀请函制作器】应用如何处理您的信息。

1. 我们收集与存储的信息

  • 您的作品：您设计/编辑的邀请函、卡片以及其中的文字、图片，均仅保存在您设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器，我们无法访问。
  • 应用设置：主题、语言等偏好保存在设备本地。
  • 我们不收集设备标识、位置、通讯录等个人信息，不内置第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK。

    • 2. 权限说明

  • 相机：拍摄照片用于卡片设计（仅经您同意时使用）
  • 照片/存储：导入图片素材与保存导出作品

    • 3. 数据的导出与删除

  • 您可在应用内随时删除已保存的作品；卸载应用即会清除设备上存储的全部数据。

    • 4. 字体加载

    应用启动时可能从 Google Fonts 下载字体并缓存于本地。

    5. 儿童隐私

    本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

    6. 政策变更

    如本政策更新，我们将在本页面更新生效日期。

    7. 联系我们

    如有隐私相关问题，请通过应用商店页面或开发者网站 https://docs.iothub.cloud 提供的联系方式与我们联系。

    English

    We value and protect your privacy. This policy explains how the Invitation Maker & Card Design app handles your information.

    1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Your designs (invitations, cards, and the text/photos you add) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, and we have no access to them.
  • App settings (theme, language) are stored locally.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location or contacts, and we include no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

    • 2. Permissions

  • Camera: take photos for card designs (only with your consent)
  • Photos/Storage: import image assets and save exported works

    • 3. Export & Deletion

  • Delete saved designs anytime from within the app; uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

4. Fonts

The app may download fonts from Google Fonts and cache them locally.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Changes

We will update the effective date on this page when the policy changes.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the developer site: https://docs.iothub.cloud
