中文

我们重视并保护您的隐私。本政策说明【简历制作】应用如何处理您的信息。

1. 我们收集与存储的信息

  • 简历与求职信内容：您输入的姓名、联系方式、工作/教育经历等所有内容，仅保存在您设备本地（本地数据库），不会上传到我们的服务器，我们无法访问。
  • 应用设置：主题、语言、默认模板等，保存在设备本地。
  • 我们不收集设备标识、位置、通讯录等个人信息，不内置第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK。

    • 2. 第三方服务（仅在您主动使用时）

  • AI 写作助手（可选）：仅当您在「设置」中配置接口并主动触发生成时，相关文本（如目标职位、要点）会发送至您所配置的 OpenAI 兼容接口。API Key 仅保存在您的设备本地，不会发送给我们。是否使用、使用哪个服务商完全由您决定。
  • 字体加载：生成 PDF 时可能从 Google Fonts 下载字体并缓存于本地。
  • 导出与分享：导出 PDF / Word / 图片或备份时，调用系统分享/打印组件，由您选择目标应用。

    • 3. 数据的导出与删除

  • 您可随时在「设置」中导出全部数据（JSON）或导入备份。
  • 卸载应用即会清除设备上存储的全部数据。

    • 4. 儿童隐私

    本应用面向求职人群，不针对 13 岁以下儿童。

    5. 政策变更

    如本政策更新，我们将在本页面更新生效日期。

    6. 联系我们

    如有隐私相关问题，请通过应用商店页面或开发者网站 https://docs.iothub.cloud 提供的联系方式与我们联系。

    English

    We value and protect your privacy. This policy explains how the Resume Builder & CV Maker app handles your information.

    1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Resume and cover letter content (name, contact details, work/education history) is stored only on your device in a local database. It is never uploaded to our servers, and we have no access to it.
  • App settings (theme, language, default template) are stored locally.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location or contacts, and we include no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

    • 2. Third-Party Services (only when you actively use them)

  • AI writing assistant (optional): only when you configure an endpoint in Settings and actively trigger generation, relevant text (target role, bullet points, etc.) is sent to the OpenAI-compatible API that you configure. Your API key stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Fonts: PDF generation may download fonts from Google Fonts and cache them locally.
  • Export/Share: PDF / Word / image export uses system share and print components that you choose.

    • 3. Export & Deletion

  • Export all data (JSON) or import a backup anytime from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.

5. Changes

We will update the effective date on this page when the policy changes.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the developer site: https://docs.iothub.cloud
